# Uplora: Market Radar & Opportunity Engine

## 1. Grounded Market Intelligence
The Market Radar continuously monitors external shifts across four critical sectors using Gemini with Google Search grounding:
1. **WhatsApp & Conversational Commerce**: Meta API updates, WhatsApp Flows, Catalog cart features, India UPI in-chat payment regulations.
2. **Instagram & Social Selling**: Changes to Instagram Shopping, bio link policies, creator commerce, DM automation limits.
3. **Indian MSME & E-Commerce Trends**: ONDC expansion, Tier-2/3 digital adoption, Indian retail digitalization, logistics courier integrations (Shiprocket, Delhivery).
4. **AI & Automation Tools**: Open source tools, API cost reductions, autonomous scraping, voice AI for regional Indian languages.

---

## 2. The Opportunity Conversion Funnel
The Market Radar prohibits uncurated news feeds. Every signal must pass through the **Uplora Relevance Filter**:

```
[External Event / News Signal]
            │
            ▼
[Relevance Filter: "Does this impact Uplora's merchants or StoreIK?"]
            │
            ▼
[Commercial Opportunity Record]
  - Source & Real-time Citation
  - Why it matters to Uplora
  - Target Customer Persona
  - Potential Uplora Offering / Feature
  - Estimated Revenue Potential
            │
            ▼
[Action Button: Convert to Idea Engine OR Create 7-Day Experiment]
```

### Example Transformation
* **Signal**: "Meta introduces WhatsApp Flow forms for Indian local businesses."
* **Uplora Translation**:
  * *Relevance*: Direct competitive advantage for Uplora's client websites.
  * *Opportunity*: Build a standardized "Instant WhatsApp Appointment / Order Booking Form" add-on for local salons, clinics, and boutique shops.
  * *Pricing*: Offer as a ₹2,999 upsell on existing client website builds.
  * *Action*: Added to Idea Engine as a 7-day validation experiment.
