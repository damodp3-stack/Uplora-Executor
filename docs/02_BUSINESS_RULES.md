# Uplora: Business Rules & Operating Principles

## 1. Capital & Revenue Rules
1. **Cash Flow is King**: No strategic expansion (such as full-time hiring or heavy R&D) occurs unless monthly cash collections exceed overhead by at least 2.5x.
2. **Current Pricing Guardrails**:
   * Standard Website: ₹6,000 – ₹10,000 (3-5 day turnaround).
   * E-commerce / Custom Catalog: ₹12,000 – ₹25,000.
   * WhatsApp Automation Add-on: ₹3,000 – ₹8,000 setup + recurring retainer.
   * StoreIK Subscription: ₹999 – ₹2,999/month (future recurring engine).
3. **No Unpaid Scope Creep**: Additional revisions or feature requests outside the original project brief must trigger an immediate change quote.

## 2. Lead & Sales Discipline
1. **Daily Hunter Quota**:
   * Assistant must identify and enrich $\ge 30$ qualified SMB prospects daily.
   * If lead yield drops below 20 for two consecutive days, an operational alert is dispatched to Damo.
2. **Founder Closing Velocity**:
   * Damo must attempt $\ge 15$ qualified outreach calls or live voice pitches daily during scaling sprints.
   * Every lead contacted must receive a structured follow-up within 48 hours until marked as 'Won' or 'Lost'.
3. **No Phantom Pipeline**: Leads stagnant in 'Proposal' or 'Negotiation' for $> 14$ days without communication are auto-flagged for disqualification or archival.

## 3. Delivery & Quality Assurance
1. **Partner SLA**: Website development projects must deliver an initial client preview within 5 business days of deposit receipt.
2. **Zero Defect Handoff**: No client site goes live without mobile responsiveness check, SSL verification, WhatsApp click-to-chat validation, and basic on-page SEO.

## 4. Idea-Hopping Protection
1. **The 7-Day Idea Quarantine**: New business pivots or side projects must be submitted to the Idea Engine. No development code may be written for 7 days while market validation (customer interviews, landing page test, or prepayments) is evaluated.
2. **Strategic Change Protocol**: Dropping a proven revenue stream (e.g., website services) to pursue an unproven bet requires a formal 14-day parallel pilot and COO review.
