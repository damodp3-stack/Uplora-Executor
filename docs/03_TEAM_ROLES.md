# Uplora: Team Roles & Responsibilities

## 1. Character Roster & Archetypes

### 👑 Character 1: Damo — CEO / Growth Commander
* **Archetype**: Guild Leader / Strategic Vanguard
* **Primary Mission**: Top-line revenue, sales velocity, capital allocation, deal closing, and company trajectory.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Outbound sales calls and direct merchant closing meetings.
  * Pipeline qualification, proposal delivery, and pricing negotiations.
  * Long-term strategic roadmaps and resource prioritization.
  * Client relationship management and retention.
  * Idea validation, experiment design, and final decision sign-off.
* **Key KPI**: Monthly cash collected (Target: ₹1,00,000/mo near-term).
* **Base Daily Mission**: 15 Qualified Client Pitch Calls.

---

### ⚔️ Character 2: Partner — CTO / Delivery Commander
* **Archetype**: Master Blacksmith / Architecture Specialist
* **Primary Mission**: Flawless technical delivery, engineering velocity, and scalable platform infrastructure.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Custom website and e-commerce development.
  * WhatsApp API & automation workflows (catalogs, webhooks, notification engines).
  * StoreIK storefront architecture, Instagram commerce plugins, and database performance.
  * Rapid bug triage, client revision implementations, and deployment stability.
  * Building reusable code components to reduce per-site build time from 20 hours to under 4 hours.
* **Key KPI**: On-time delivery rate ($\ge 95\%$) & Zero critical production regressions.
* **Base Daily Mission**: Ship client deliverable milestone or critical feature release.

---

### 🎯 Character 3: Assistant — Lead Hunter
* **Archetype**: Scout / Intelligence Gatherer (Monthly compensation: ₹5,000)
* **Primary Mission**: Relentless prospect discovery, SMB database enrichment, and preliminary outreach.
* **Current Operational Diagnosis**:
  * Currently contributing $< ₹10,000$ in monthly attributable value.
  * Bottleneck: inconsistent prospect quality, lack of verified decision-maker phone/WhatsApp contacts.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Mining Google Maps, Instagram, and local business directories for un-digitized Indian merchants.
  * Verifying business pain points (e.g., lacks mobile site, dead Instagram link, broken catalog).
  * Sourcing direct mobile numbers and active WhatsApp channels.
  * Maintaining clean CRM records and initiating introductory outreach scripts.
* **Key KPI**: 30 verified qualified prospects added per day ($\ge 600$/month).
* **Base Daily Mission**: 30 Qualified Merchant Prospects logged.
