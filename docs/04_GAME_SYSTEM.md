# Uplora: Game System & Progression Architecture

## 1. XP (Experience Points) Economy
The XP system strictly avoids rewarding shallow vanity metrics. Only high-leverage commercial and execution milestones mint XP.

| Event Type | XP Awarded | Criteria & Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Collected** | 1 XP per ₹100 | Directly tied to banked customer payments (e.g., ₹10,000 deal = 100 XP) |
| **New Client Closed** | +250 XP | First contract or payment cleared for a new business client |
| **Client Site / System Shipped**| +180 XP | On-time delivery meeting all checklist standards |
| **Qualified Lead Logged** | +5 XP | Prospect verified with phone, WhatsApp, and identified pain point |
| **Cold Call / Live Pitch** | +15 XP | Meaningful dialogue with a decision maker |
| **Proposal Sent** | +50 XP | Formally structured quote delivered to qualified prospect |
| **Daily Streak Multiplier** | +10% XP/day | Up to 1.5x cap at a 5-day continuous execution streak |
| **Validated Experiment** | +200 XP | A 7-day hypothesis with recorded customer metrics & decision |
| **Process Improvement** | +150 XP | Shipping a reusable component or automation template |
| **Daily Check-in Completed** | +25 XP | Submitting the 8-question operational reflection |
| **Monthly Boss Battle Cleared**| +500 XP | Meeting or exceeding monthly revenue benchmark |

## 2. Level Progression System (Level 1 to 10)
Levels bridge the gap from a fragile ₹0 startup to a ₹1,00,00,00,000 (₹100 Crore / ₹1B) Commerce-Tech empire.

* **Level 1 — Startup Survivor (₹0 → ₹1,00,000 cumulative / run-rate milestone)**
  * *Threshold*: 0 – 1,000 XP
  * *Perk*: Basic CRM, Daily Quests, AI Task Adaptation unlocked.
* **Level 2 — Revenue Builder (₹1,00,000 → ₹5,00,000)**
  * *Threshold*: 1,000 – 3,500 XP
  * *Perk*: Weekly Planning Engine, Deal Health Analyzer.
* **Level 3 — Growth Operator (₹5,00,000 → ₹10,00,000)**
  * *Threshold*: 3,500 – 8,000 XP
  * *Perk*: Market Radar & Google Search Grounding Intelligence.
* **Level 4 — Business Engine (₹10,00,000 → ₹25,00,000)**
  * *Threshold*: 8,000 – 18,000 XP
  * *Perk*: Automated Client Retention Tracking & Churn Radar.
* **Level 5 — Commerce-Tech Company (₹25,00,000 → ₹50,00,000)**
  * *Threshold*: 18,000 – 35,000 XP
  * *Perk*: StoreIK Merchant Onboarding Engine & Multi-Tier Analytics.
* **Level 6 — Scale Mode (₹50,00,000 → ₹1,00,00,000 / ₹1 Crore)**
  * *Threshold*: 35,00,000 – 75,000 XP
* **Level 7 — Multi-Product Vanguard (₹1 Crore → ₹5 Crore)**
  * *Threshold*: 75,000 – 150,000 XP
* **Level 8 — Enterprise Velocity (₹5 Crore → ₹20 Crore)**
  * *Threshold*: 150,000 – 350,000 XP
* **Level 9 — Industry Dominator (₹20 Crore → ₹50 Crore)**
  * *Threshold*: 350,000 – 750,000 XP
* **Level 10 — ₹1B Empire (₹100 Crore / ₹1,000,000,000)**
  * *Threshold*: 1,000,000+ XP

## 3. Achievement Badges (Trophy Vault)
* 🏅 **The First Rupee**: Bank first ₹1,000 payment from a real client.
* 🏅 **Ten-K Milestone**: Clear ₹10,000 in monthly revenue.
* 🏅 **Half-Century Club**: Clear ₹50,000 in monthly cash collected.
* 🏅 **Six-Figure Operator**: First ₹1,00,000 month!
* 🏅 **Deca-Client**: Onboard 10 active paying accounts.
* 🏅 **Iron Will (5-Day Streak)**: 5 consecutive days of 100% core daily quest completion.
* 🏅 **Titanium Focus (20-Day Streak)**: 20 consecutive days of operational check-ins.
* 🏅 **First StoreIK Merchant**: Close the first customer for the StoreIK platform.
* 🏅 **SaaS Recurring Pioneer**: First recurring monthly subscription billed and collected.
* 🏅 **The 1 Crore Milestone**: ₹1,00,00,000 cumulative revenue achieved.
* 🏆 **1B Conquest**: The ultimate trophy — ₹1,000,000,000 achieved.
